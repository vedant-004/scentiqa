# Sol de Janeiro Cheirosa Intense: Four New Mists, Higher Concentration, 10-Hour Wear Claim

The mists that built a cult just got stronger. On 12 October 2026, Sol de Janeiro launched the **Cheirosa Intense** collection: higher-concentration takes on four of its most loved scents, Cheirosa 62, 59, 91 and 74. The brand's headline claim is up to 10 hours of wear, a big jump for a format that has always been about light, refreshing sprays rather than all-day performance.

That claim is the brand's, not ours. Nobody outside the brand has worn these for a full day yet. But the concentration bump is real, and for mist lovers it is the most meaningful change Sol de Janeiro has made to this line.

## What is actually new

The original Cheirosa mists are beloved for being easy, breezy and affordable, but they fade fast. That is the nature of a body mist: low fragrance oil concentration, lots of wear-everywhere charm, gone by lunch. The Intense collection keeps the same sunny DNA and raises the oil concentration, which should mean stronger projection and longer life on skin.

Pricing is straightforward: **$34 for 90ml in the US, £34 at Boots in the UK, and €33.95 on Zalando**. India pricing has not been announced yet, and there is no confirmed India launch date, so do not trust any INR price you see floating around until an official retailer lists it.

## The four scents

### Cheirosa 62 Intense: the icon, turned up

The original 62 is the pistachio-vanilla scent that made the brand famous. The Intense version is described as a sun-drenched gourmand built on **roasted pistachio, vanilla absolute and a solar ambery accord**.

The full verified pyramid: pistachio and almond up top; heliotrope, jasmine and roasted macadamia in the heart; solar ambery accord, vanilla absolute, salted caramel, sandalwood and patchouli in the base. If you love the original, this is the same story with the volume raised and a warmer, longer drydown.

### Cheirosa 59 Intense: the euphoric gourmand

Built on **white orchid, creamy sandalwood and the solar ambery accord**. The brand has announced only these featured notes so far, so the full pyramid is still unconfirmed. Expect the dreamy, velvety character of the original 59 with more staying power.

### Cheirosa 91 Intense: the floral one

The most complex of the four, with a full pyramid announced. A floral-fruity mist: **bergamot, passion fruit, cashew, lychee and acai** up top; **magnolia, Rio pink rose, pistachio and dewy peony** in the heart; **solar ambery accord, liquid musk and pink sugar** at the base. This is the pick for anyone who found the original line too sweet and one-dimensional.

### Cheirosa 74 Intense: the golden gourmand

The newcomer of the bunch, built on **ripe pineapple, orange flower and the solar ambery accord**. Like the 59, only featured notes have been announced so far. Tropical, golden and unabashedly fun.

## What Indian buyers should know

A few honest notes before you get excited:

**Mists and Indian heat are friends.** Light sprays work beautifully in hot, humid weather, where heavy perfumes can turn cloying. If you want more on picking scents for Indian summers, our [guide to long-lasting summer perfumes](/blog/best-long-lasting-perfumes-indian-summer-2026) covers the logic in detail.

**Wait for the wear tests.** The 10-hour claim is marketing until independent wear tests exist. Mists live or die on real-world longevity, and we will update this post when verified numbers come in.

**Watch for fakes.** Every hyped launch spawns counterfeits within weeks, and mist bottles are easy to fake. Buy from official retailers only, and if a deal looks too good, it is. Our [guide to spotting fakes in India](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-perfumes-india) shows exactly what to check.

**India pricing is unconfirmed.** We will add verified INR prices and buy links the moment an official Indian retailer lists the collection. Anything you see before that is speculation.

## The bottom line

The Cheirosa Intense collection is Sol de Janeiro fixing the one real complaint about its mists: they never lasted. Higher concentration, the same beloved DNA, and a fair $34 price point make this an easy recommendation for mist fans. Whether the 10-hour wear claim survives contact with real skin is the open question, and it is the one we will be watching.
