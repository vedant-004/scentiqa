# Scentarchy 2-Month SEO Battle Plan
### 16 October – 16 December 2026 | Goal: Maximum Google rankings

---

## Ground Rules (Strict)

1. **No black-hat, ever.** No bought links, no PBNs, no link farms, no keyword stuffing. Google's SpamBrain kills sites for this. One penalty wipes out 2 months of work.
2. **Publish on schedule, no excuses.** Content velocity is a ranking signal. Miss a week and you lose momentum.
3. **Every piece of content must be genuinely useful.** Thin filler hurts more than it helps.
4. **Track everything weekly** in Search Console. What gets measured gets improved.
